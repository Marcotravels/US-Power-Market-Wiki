# 配电 (Power Distribution)

配电是将电力从变电站输送到最终用户（家庭、企业）的过程。

## 配电流程

1. **变电站** → 电压从高压降到中压 (4kV - 34kV)
2. **配电线路** → 电线杆或地下电缆
3. **配电变压器** → 电压降到用户可用级别 (120V / 240V)
4. **用户端** → 进入家庭/建筑物

## 电压等级

| 阶段 | 典型电压 |
|------|----------|
| 输电 | 115kV - 765kV |
| 配电 (一次) | 4kV - 34kV |
| 配电 (二次) | 120V - 240V |

## 配电系统组成

- **架空线路**: 电线杆上的线路
- **地下电缆**: 城市中心区
- **配电变压器**: 安装在电线杆上或地面上
- **电表**: 记录用户用电量

## 电力供应商类型

美国电力市场有多种供应商：

1. **市政公用事业** (Municipal) - 非营利，由市政府所有
2. **电力合作社** (Cooperative) - 由成员所有
3. **投资者所有的公用事业** (IOU) - 私人营利性公司，如 PG&E, Duke Energy
4. **联邦所有** - TVA, BPA 等

# English Version

## Power Distribution

Power distribution is the process of delivering electricity from substations to end users (homes, businesses).

## Distribution Process

1. **Substation** → Voltage steps down from high to medium voltage (4kV - 34kV)
2. **Distribution lines** → Overhead poles or underground cables
3. **Distribution transformer** → Voltage steps down to usable level (120V / 240V)
4. **Customer premises** → Enters homes/buildings

## Voltage Levels

| Stage | Typical Voltage |
|-------|----------------|
| Transmission | 115kV - 765kV |
| Distribution (primary) | 4kV - 34kV |
| Distribution (secondary) | 120V - 240V |

## Distribution System Components

- **Overhead lines**: Lines on utility poles
- **Underground cables**: Urban centers
- **Distribution transformers**: Mounted on poles or at ground level
- **Meters**: Record customer electricity consumption

## Types of Electricity Suppliers

The U.S. electricity market has multiple supplier types:

1. **Municipal utilities** - Non-profit, owned by city government
2. **Cooperatives** - Member-owned
3. **Investor-Owned Utilities (IOU)** - Private for-profit companies, e.g., PG&E, Duke Energy
4. **Federally owned** - TVA, BPA, etc.
