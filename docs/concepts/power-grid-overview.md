# 电力电网概述

电力电网是连接电力生产商和消费者的复杂网络系统。它包括变电站、变压器和电力线路。

## 电网的三个层次

### 1. 发电 (Generation)

电力在发电厂产生。发电厂使用各种一次能源（煤炭、天然气、核能、太阳能、风能）驱动发电机，将机械能转化为电能。

→ 详见 [[power-generation]]

### 2. 输电 (Transmission)

高压输电线路承载电力跨越长距离。从发电厂到变电站，电压通常很高（几十万千伏），以减少传输损耗。

→ 详见 [[power-transmission]]

### 3. 配电 (Distribution)

电力从变电站通过配电线路输送到家庭和企业。电压在变电站和配电变压器处降低到安全水平（120V/240V）。

→ 详见 [[power-distribution]]

## 关键特征

| 特征 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 实时平衡 | 发电量必须与用电量实时匹配 |
| 互联互通 | 多个发电厂和电网互相连接，提供冗余 |
| 可靠性 | 冗余设计防止大范围停电 |

## 美国电网的独特性

美国电网是世界上最大的互联电网之一，由三个主要独立系统组成：
- [[three-major-interconnections|东部互联（Eastern Interconnection）]]
- [[three-major-interconnections|西部互联（Western Interconnection）]]
- [[three-major-interconnections|ERCOT（德州互联）]]

这三个系统基本独立运行，很少相互转移电力。

# English Version

## Power Grid Overview

The electric power grid is a complex network system connecting electricity producers and consumers. It comprises substations, transformers, and power lines.

## The Three Layers of the Grid

### 1. Generation

Electricity is produced at power plants. Power plants use various primary energy sources (coal, natural gas, nuclear, solar, wind) to drive generators, converting mechanical energy into electrical energy.

→ See [[power-generation]]

### 2. Transmission

High-voltage transmission lines carry electricity over long distances. From power plants to substations, voltage is typically very high (hundreds of kilovolts) to reduce transmission losses.

→ See [[power-transmission]]

### 3. Distribution

Electricity flows from substations through distribution lines to homes and businesses. Voltage is reduced to safe levels (120V/240V) at substations and distribution transformers.

→ See [[power-distribution]]

## Key Characteristics

| Characteristic | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Real-time balance | Power generated must match power consumed at every moment |
| Interconnection | Multiple power plants and grids are interconnected, providing redundancy |
| Reliability | Redundant design prevents large-scale blackouts |

## The Uniqueness of the U.S. Power Grid

The U.S. power grid is one of the largest interconnected systems in the world, consisting of three major independent systems:
- [[three-major-interconnections|Eastern Interconnection]]
- [[three-major-interconnections|Western Interconnection]]
- [[three-major-interconnections|ERCOT (Texas Interconnection)]]

These three systems operate largely independently, with minimal power transfer between them.
